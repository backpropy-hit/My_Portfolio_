## Problem
Control a robot with hand gestures seen by a camera, with no remote.

## What I built
- A YOLOv11x model that recognises 8 gesture classes, trained for 50 epochs on 2,000+ images with augmentation for varied hand positions and backgrounds.
- An OpenCV pipeline for image processing.
- ESP32 firmware (Arduino IDE) for motor control.

## Result
End-to-end latency from detection to movement under 100 ms.
