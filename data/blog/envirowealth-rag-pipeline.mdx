## Problem
EnviroWealth, a carbon sustainability startup, needed to generate BRSR sustainability reports from large, messy source documents.

## What I worked on
My internship ran May to July 2026. I contributed to a RAG-based pipeline with three stages:
- OCR of scanned documents with Tesseract
- Chunking with Docling
- Storage and retrieval in a Qdrant vector database

I also built a standalone chatbot prototype with Streamlit and the Gemini API. It lets a user upload a report and ask questions about it, to explore conversational report generation. It stayed at prototype stage.

**Tools:** Python, Tesseract, Docling, Qdrant, LangChain, Streamlit, Gemini API
