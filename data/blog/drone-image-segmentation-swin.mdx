## Problem
Drone photos need to be split into meaningful regions so each part of the scene can be analysed separately.

## What I built
A Swin Transformer segmentation model trained on a drone dataset. It labels every pixel as one of seven classes: other, wall, road, vegetation, vehicle, roof and water. I wrapped it in a Streamlit app where you upload an aerial image and see the segmented result.

## Result
mIoU: 84.05

**Tools:** PyTorch, Swin Transformer, Streamlit
