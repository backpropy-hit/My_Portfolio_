## Problem
The IRC'26 Reconnaissance and Delivery Operation (RADO) mission needed the rover to detect and classify cones on its own and navigate to them.

## What I built
- Expanded a Kaggle dataset from 4 to 6 cone classes with HSV-based synthetic generation, then trained a custom YOLOv8 model that held up across different lighting.
- Converted the model with OpenVINO for CPU-only deployment on an Intel NUC, which removed a real-time latency bottleneck.
- Built ArUco marker detection and pose estimation for the autonomous mission.
- Deployed the detector as a ROS2 node that streams cone coordinates to the navigation stack.

## Result
The team ranked 9th internationally at IRC'26 and was the only NIT team representing in that ranking.
